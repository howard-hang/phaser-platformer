方块跑酷隐私政策

应用名：方块跑酷（Block Runner）。包名：com.fangkuai.paoku。本政策说明这款游戏在你的设备上保存什么，以及广告合作方会处理什么。

生效日期：2026-10-03。我们没有自己的账号系统，也没有自己的服务器来接收你的存档。

存在设备上的数据

闯关星星、解锁进度、无尽最高纪录、音量、震动、特效、帧率显示和界面语言，都写在这台设备的本地存储里。这些数据不会上传给我们。你可以在游戏设置里「重置进度」，或在系统里清除应用数据、卸载应用，把它们删掉。

广告与广告 ID

安卓版在死亡后可以观看一条激励视频来复活。广告由 Google AdMob 提供。应用 ID 为 ca-app-pub-3218611878548189~9810569030，激励广告位为 revive_rewarded。

为了展示、衡量和防止广告欺诈，Google Mobile Ads SDK 可能收集并与 Google 共享：

  • 广告 ID（Advertising ID，Android 的广告标识）
  • 设备信息，例如型号、系统版本、语言
  • 由 IP 地址推断的大致位置。应用不申请精确位置权限
  • 广告互动，例如是否展示、是否看完激励视频

我们自己拿不到这些明细。处理方式以 Google 的说明为准：Google 隐私权政策，以及 AdMob 与用户隐私。

你可以在 Android 设置里重置或删除广告 ID，或在系统的广告设置里选择退出个性化广告。

欧洲和英国的同意（UMP）

在欧洲经济区和英国，Google 的 User Messaging Platform（UMP）会在需要时弹出同意框，让你选择是否允许用数据来做个性化广告。你的选择由 Google 的 SDK 保存在本机。关掉同意框、同意接口失败，或你所在地区不需要这份同意时，游戏仍会尝试请求广告；能否使用广告 ID，由 Google SDK 按当时的同意结果决定。

儿童

Block Runner Privacy Policy

App name: Block Runner (方块跑酷). Package: com.fangkuai.paoku. This policy explains what stays on your device and what the advertising partner processes.

Effective date: 2026-10-03. There is no account system and no developer server that receives your save data.

Data stored on the device

Level stars, unlocks, your endless best, volume, vibration, effects, the FPS toggle, and the interface language are stored locally on the device. We do not upload them. Use Reset Progress in Settings, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app to delete them.

Ads and the advertising ID

On Android, after you fall you can watch one rewarded video to revive. Ads are served by Google AdMob. The app ID is ca-app-pub-3218611878548189~9810569030 and the rewarded unit is revive_rewarded.

To show, measure, and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • the Advertising ID
  • device details such as model, OS version, and language
  • approximate location inferred from the IP address. The app does not request precise location
  • ad interactions, such as whether a rewarded video was shown or completed

We do not receive those records ourselves. See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data in AdMob.

You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings, and you can opt out of personalized ads there.

Consent in the EEA and the UK (UMP)

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) shows a consent form when one is required. Your choice is stored on the device by Google’s SDK. If you dismiss the form, the consent request fails, or your region does not require consent, the game still tries to request an ad. Whether the advertising ID may be used is decided by the Google SDK from the consent state.

Children

Block Runner is not directed at children and is not directed at anyone under 13. Do not use this app to provide a child’s personal information.
